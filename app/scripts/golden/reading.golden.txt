## M-LENS 日主 rì zhǔ — the lens
   cites: F-EIGHT, F-DAYMASTER, F-SCHOOL, F-ZODIAC
   Eight characters, four pillars: 庚子 己卯 己亥 丁卯. The lens is 己 — jǐ, 土 earth, yin: field; soil that grows what is planted. Every line below is read from that one seat. The same eight characters around a different day stem would describe a different plate entirely.
   The hour is read on the true solar time school (clock time converted to 05:36 solar), and the year animal is 鼠.

## M-STANDING 强弱 qiáng ruò — where it stands
   cites: F-DELING, F-ROOTLESS, F-DEDI, F-DESHI, F-XINGYUN-YEAR, F-XINGYUN-MONTH, F-XINGYUN-DAY, F-XINGYUN-HOUR, F-STRENGTH
   The month branch is 卯. For 己 that season reads 死 — the season controls it — dormant, working against the grain of the month; the plate does not command the season (失令).
   Underneath, nothing answers: no branch carries 己's element as a hidden stem. The tradition calls this rootless — a stem standing in the open, with the whole question of support thrown onto the season and the company it keeps.
   Of the three other visible stems, 2 support and 1 draws off: 庚 in the year pillar reads 伤官, 己 in the month pillar reads 比肩, and 丁 in the hour pillar reads 偏印.
   Read through the twelve stages — 十二长生, a life cycle laid over every stem-and-branch pairing — the day master sits at 绝 in 子 (the year branch), 病 in 卯 (the month branch), 胎 in 亥 (自坐, its own seat), and 病 in 卯 (the hour branch). The engine uses those stages as a finer supplement to the roots above. The tradition reads them as phases of a cycle — 长生 through 墓 and round again — not as a scorecard, and the schools do not agree on whether the yin stems run the cycle backwards.
   Blended — half the weight on the season, a third on the roots, a fifth on the company — the plate lands 身弱 at -0.45. The weights and the arithmetic are printed on the chart page; the judgment is a documented heuristic, not a classical formula, and it is meant to be argued with.

## M-SHAPE 格局 gé jú — the shape it makes
   cites: F-PATTERN
   The month's presiding god is 七杀, carried by the hidden stem 乙 — which names this plate a 七杀格. A 七杀格 wants its pressure channelled rather than removed — the weight is the engine, if something carries it.
   Pattern comes before medicine: it sets what the plate is trying to do, and the favorable god below is chosen in service of that, not in isolation.

## M-TENSION 关系 guān xì — where it pulls
   cites: F-REL-1, F-REL-2, F-REL-3, F-REL-4, F-VOID
   The branches pull on each other in 4 places: 子卯 刑 (year–month branches), 子卯 刑 (year–hour branches), 卯亥 半合 (month–day branches), and 亥卯 半合 (day–hour branches).
   No pillar sits in a void seat (the day pillar's 旬 is 甲午, voiding 辰巳).
   These are relations, not omens. The computation carries no verdict about fortune at all.

## M-IMAGERY 纳音 nà yīn — how it sounds
   cites: F-TONE-YEAR, F-TONE-MONTH, F-TONE-DAY, F-TONE-HOUR
   The 纳音 are the tradition's most lyrical layer: each pillar's ganzhi carries a phase-name that situates it somewhere — in the sea, on the rampart, under the lamp. A flavor, not a fortune.
   The year pillar 庚子 sounds 壁上土 — earth on the wall: earth pressed flat into plaster — soil made a surface. The month pillar 己卯 sounds 城头土 — earth on the rampart: soil piled into a wall — earth given the shape of a defense. The day pillar 己亥 sounds 平地木 — wood on level ground: a tree with no slope to lean on, standing by its own trunk. The hour pillar 丁卯 sounds 炉中火 — fire in the furnace: flame with walls around it — heat that is contained and put to work.
   Across the four pillars the tones run 2×土 · 1×木 · 1×火. Read them as a register the plate sounds in, not as four more verdicts stacked on the eight characters.

## M-WEATHER 大运 dà yùn — the weather over it
   cites: F-QIYUN, F-DECADES, F-DECADE-NOW, F-DECADE-REL
   The fixed plate above does not move. Over it runs the 大运 — decade pillars, beginning 1968 (7y 11m 10d after birth), then turning every ten years.
   The decade covering 2026 is 乙酉 (2018–2027): its stem 乙 reads 七杀 to the day master; its branch is 酉.
   That decade's branch meets the month branch as 酉卯 冲 — a relation between the moving layer and the plate's strongest seat.
   The timeline says what SHAPE the weather has — which stem, which branch, which relation to the plate. It does not say whether the weather is good. That word is not in the computation, and adding it would be a different kind of claim than anything this engine can check.
