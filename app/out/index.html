a language for character — and the medicine it asks for

Bazi usually arrives as fortune-telling. This center is for something else. A chart is a vocabulary for temperament — 五行 elements, 十神 ten gods, 藏干 hidden stems — precise enough to say what a person is actually like, which “introvert” never manages. Two things follow:

  1. A framework for character — yours and other people's, named in the same terms, so difference stops reading as defect. Not to sort people, but to see them.
  2. The medicine each of us needs — what runs hot needs different care from what runs dry, and what steadies you can starve someone else. Not a forecast; a prescription pad for attention.

a Christian frame, stated up front

God first — before the chart, and over it. Four commitments:

  • We do not worship the stars. No prayer or offering to a pillar, a planet, or a phase. 2 Kings 18:4: Hezekiah smashed the bronze serpent once people burned incense to it — it had healed, it was never meant to be adored.
  • We read pattern in creation, nothing more. 气 and the 五行 it moves through are created substances — like light, like water — not spirits, not powers. The heavens declare the glory of God (Ps 19); they are not God.
  • The good are not bound by fate. 善人不为命所缚. A chart maps the temperament God gave you; it describes, it does not sentence.
  • Every chart has a rebirth slot. 重生 — John 3:3. Enter the day you were reborn in Christ and the 大运 timeline is marked there: the same weather, falling on a new creation (2 Cor 5:17).

Where chart and Scripture disagree, Scripture wins. Nothing computed here has authority over your standing before God. Follow the Lion, not the chart (Rev 5:5).

what the chart page gives you

  • Your full four-pillar chart, computed in your browser — nothing leaves it until you submit.
  • A rebirth slot: the 大运 decade timeline marked at the day you were reborn in Christ.
  • 合婚 pair reading — two computed layers, no verdict.
  • Every warning the engine can give: boundary honesty near a 节, unknown-hour handling, both hour schools.

It also makes the system testable — a thousand years of practice, almost no record of anyone checking whether it works. Every chart is computed deterministically (VSOP87D solar terms, a pinned Python oracle, byte-matched in CI), so the same birth data gives the same chart anywhere. Entering yours joins the study: each chart becomes a research record, held under covenant, deletable, feeding preregistered outcome surveys. Nulls get published like hits. If Bazi carries real signal a public commons is how anyone finds out; if it doesn't, it should say so plainly.
