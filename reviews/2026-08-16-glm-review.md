# Review: Bazi Literacy Platform Plan (bazillionaire)

Reviewer: honest-critic mode. No cheerleading. Odds are calibrated against a real bar, not "covers costs for a solo founder with near-zero cash burn" — that bar is a hobby threshold wearing a business suit.

---

## 1. Sharpest structural objections (the things that could kill it)

### 1a. The plan demotes its best bet and promotes its worst
The prior riff (2026-08-13) rated the 合婚 (hé hūn, marriage-compatibility) decision tool at 40–60% viable, with a recommended MVP of "days not months, charge from day one." This plan takes that wedge — the only component with demonstrated willingness-to-pay logic (parents, pre-marriage pressure, $49–99 price point) — and demotes it to "report type, not new interface," while promoting literacy-first pedagogy (claimed 35%, and I'd say 20–25%) to the venture. That is backwards sequencing. 合婚 is where money and ritual pressure already exist; literacy is a hypothesis about demand that has never been tested. One engine, two surfaces is the right architecture — but the order of surfaces should follow the money, and this plan inverts it.

### 1b. The "sharpest claimed observation" is half-wrong, and it's load-bearing
"Charts are becoming free; pedagogy is not" correctly identifies that verdict-slop is a commodity. But the conclusion — that pedagogy is the moat — doesn't hold. LLMs are also the best ad-hoc explainers ever built: a competitor can wrap a chat model around a free chart and produce per-character pinyin tables, socratic dialogs, progressive explanations, on demand, for marginal cost. What an LLM cannot do is **guarantee the computation**: it hallucinates 节气 (jié qì, solar-term) boundaries, invents 藏干 (cáng gān, hidden stems), and cannot keep its claims consistent with a deterministic graph. The durable moat is *verifiable correctness made visible* — which is exactly the asset you already own. Pedagogy is the delivery format, not the moat. Misnaming this matters because it drives investment toward content production (hundreds of glossary entries) instead of toward making the verification visible (anchors, forks, auditability). Rewrite the moat sentence: **verified computation + claim-graph consistency, delivered in a teaching format.**

### 1c. The MVP contains no money and no distribution
MVP = landing → intake → chart → reading, "no account, no transit." Note what's outside the MVP: both distribution engines named in the plan (glossary SEO, gift-forwarding via the 满月 mǎn yuè poster) AND the only priced product (合婚 report). So the MVP measures engagement — the metric of the toy economics the plan itself rejects. Six months of beautiful pedagogy, four-figure traffic, zero revenue, is the most likely death: the content-product trap.

### 1d. Founder projection disguised as market research
The lexicon format is your signature pedagogy — per-character tables are how *you* think. The heritage diaspora at ritual moments does not want to learn a 22-character grammar; they want the artifact that connects them to grandma: the poster, the report, the decoded verdict. The plan itself concedes this by naming ritual moments as "distribution" — i.e., the buying moments are verdict/artifact moments, and literacy is the content layer around them. The plan then hides this concession from itself.

## 2. What's missing (load-bearing absences)

1. **A payment surface.** Nine surfaces, zero price tags. No pricing ladder, no checkout, no answer to "what does anyone pay for, where, when." The prior riff had $49–99 reports; this plan dropped it without replacement.
2. **A content-production plan for the claimed moat.** If pedagogy is the moat, the moat is mostly editorial: 22 干支 (gān zhī, stems+branches) cards are days; but 10 十神 (shí shén, Ten Gods) entries, 合/冲/刑/害 interaction entries, 神煞 (shén shà, symbolic stars) definitions, curriculum prose — that's hundreds of entries in the per-character table format. Months of part-time writing, uncosted, unnamed.
3. **A re-engagement rail.** The 大运/流年 (dà yùn/liú nián, decade/annual transit) timeline is claimed as "the recurring-engagement surface" — but the URL-as-document discipline plus smallest-account choice means: no user, no email, no notification. Gift-by-link forwards once, at Chinese New Year, only if a human remembers. The claim is structurally undermined by two other claims in the same plan. Fix is tiny: consent-gated "email me at 立春 (lì chūn, Start of Spring)" — one email/year.
4. **Success instruments.** No metrics named anywhere. What tells you in 90 days whether the wedge works? (% of readers reaching progressive layer 3; poster forwards per buyer; 合婚 report conversion rate; glossary-entry → chart-view rate.)
5. **Clock-vs-solar-time fork in intake.** The verified stack handles both hour schools and solar time; the intake spec mentions timezone/DST but not the 北京时间 vs 真太阳时 fork — for mainland-born parents and grandparents this is a real correctness fork and a trust moment. Your own chart memory marks it (clock; solar 壬子). Surface it in the form.
6. **Minors' data posture.** 满月 charts are newborns' birth data. Parents consent for a poster, fine — but write the one-paragraph policy before the first baby chart is stored, not after.
7. **The landing-page demo chart.** "Live sample chart fully annotated" — whose? Celebrity/public-data chart or bust. Family corpus charts must never leak into the demo.
8. **Practitioner channel (audience C) vanished.** The prior riff's fork B — white-label the engine to working 命理师 who hand-compute badly and get asked 合婚 daily — is absent from this plan, which dismisses C as "small, pays for rigor." That's the same instinct as "literacy," with actual money attached.

## 3. Weakest elements, by name

- **"Literacy-first" as the product fork for THIS audience.** Demand inversion: you're selling grammar to people who buy artifacts. Weakest single decision in the plan.
- **合婚 demoted to "report type."** Right architecture, wrong sequence; it removes the only payment test from the critical path.
- **MVP scope (landing → intake → chart → reading).** No payment, no distribution mechanism. Tests the wrong hypothesis.
- **Glossary as "the SEO acquisition surface."** "what is 七杀 (qī shā, Seven Killings)" long-tail is real but thin in English; head terms (bazi calculator, chinese astrology compatibility) are dominated and you will not outrank them. SEO is a 6–12 month layer, not an acquisition surface for an MVP. Fine as a channel — mislabeled as the channel.
- **十神 radial network as "product-defining."** Over-claimed. Radial graphs read poorly for non-technical users; the annotated 四柱 (sì zhù, four-pillar) grid + tap-to-card + branch-interaction arcs does the actual teaching. The radial network is a screenshot asset and a landing-page hero, not a learning tool.
- **Element-balance "temperature" gauge for 调候 (tiáo hòu, climate adjustment).** A single scalar gauge invites exactly the "your chart is 60% fire" simplification the product defines itself against, and the weight is school-dependent. If you ship it, ship it with the school visible.
- **The 35% odds.** Over-confident by 10–15 points (decomposition below) — or "right" only because the bar is low.
- **九-surface inventory vs "smallest surface" discipline.** The inventory reads like a roadmap for a team; you are one person. The plan contradicts its own discipline.

## 4. Strongest elements (only those that earn it)

- **The verified engine + the hard rule** ("every edge/glow/bar renders from deterministic output"). This is the actual moat, it exists, and it makes every other claim in the product cheap to honor — including auditability.
- **节气 boundary fork warning in intake** ("born 3h before month changed — here's the fork") + missing-hour honesty showing which pillar changes. The single best trust artifact in the plan: concrete, rare among competitors, demonstrable in one screenshot. This is the "computed, not generated" claim made visible.
- **URL-as-document structure** (no login to view, print-to-poster, gift-by-link). Correct structural insight for diaspora forwarding moments; cheap; genuinely differentiated from app-store incumbents.
- **Anti-cult posture as compliance frame** (weather + equipment, never sentences; no health/death/money certainty; consent + deletion). Risk management at the identity level; also the honest differentiation from Co-Star-grade anxiety merchandising.
- **One engine, two surfaces** (合婚 as report type, not separate venture). The consolidation itself is right — my objection is sequencing, not architecture.

## 5. Is heritage-literacy the right wedge? Odds decomposition

Claimed: ~35% niche-viable. Decompose (18-month horizon, bar = $2k/mo sustained):

| Component | My estimate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Heritage segment exists with felt literacy loss | 0.70 | Real cultural phenomenon (ABC illiteracy, unread charts in drawers) |
| Reachable by solo founder (SEO + ritual forwarding) | 0.45 | Thin English long-tail; head terms dominated; forwarding needs the poster product, which is outside MVP |
| Willing to PAY for literacy per se | 0.25 | Nobody has ever shown this; education for its own sake converts at hobbyist rates |
| Willing to pay at ritual/artifact moments (poster, 合婚 report) | 0.55 | Institutional durability of 合婚; prior riff's evidence |
| Solo-founder execution across engine + content + SEO + support | 0.50 | The 9-surface inventory is a team plan |

Joint for literacy-first as sequenced: 0.70 × 0.45 × 0.25 × 0.50 ≈ **4%** at a strict reading — too harsh, because literacy content partially feeds artifact conversion. Realistic call: **20–25%** niche-viable as the plan is sequenced.

**The better first customer hiding in plain sight: the ritual buyer the plan treats as "distribution."** Concretely, two:

1. **The pre-marriage couple / parent in the 合婚 moment** — the Western or ABC partner confronting the tradition, Googling at the exact moment of maximum urgency. Prior riff: 40–60%. This plan defers it.
2. **The "decode the drawer" customer:** the family that already HAS the chart — grandma's paper, the temple poster, the master's scrawl nobody can read — and wants it decoded into the lexicon format with the per-character tables. "Decode the artifact" (paid translation-with-rigor of an existing family object, $19–39 + poster upsell) sits exactly between heritage sentiment and pedagogy, uses the identical engine and cards, and is a *smaller* build than progressive literacy — no curriculum needed, one intake path (photo/manual entry of an existing chart). This is the wedge the plan doesn't see: literacy as a *service* before literacy as a *product*.

My calibration: as-written literacy-first, **20–25%**. Same assets resequenced (合婚 report + decode-the-drawer + poster first, literacy surfaces as the content layer), **~40%** — consistent with the prior riff, discounted for solo bandwidth and unverified competitive landscape.

## 6. Auditable reading: moat or month-long detour?

Cost, honestly: if the reading is template-generated from the computed graph — which the hard rule already mandates — then claim→edge linkage is data plumbing: each claim template carries an ID referencing the graph edge/weight. That's **~1 week for the top 20 claims**, not a month. The month version only exists if the reading text is free-form LLM prose, which the hard rule forbids anyway.

Payoff: it is the *evidence layer* of the moat, not the moat. Competitors can copy clickable edges in a quarter; they cannot copy the verified engine underneath that makes the clicks true. So auditability is what makes "computed, not generated" demonstrable rather than assertable — that's worth a week and a landing-page demo, permanently. Ship it for the reading's headline claims, not all claims. **Verdict: cheap conditional on discipline you've already committed to; do it, but don't call it the moat.**

## 7. Top 5 recommendations, sequenced (30 days)

1. **Week 1–2 — ship the paid 合婚 report as a thin service.** Form → two charts → three-layer report on the existing verified engine. Stripe, $49–99, manual fulfillment by email PDF is fine. This is the payment test the MVP lacks. Days-not-months per the prior riff; the only new build is intake + checkout + one report template.
2. **Week 1–2 — publish the methodology page.** The published anchors, the family-corpus regression method, the 节气 fork screenshot, both hour schools, solar-vs-clock policy. This is the trust artifact, the PR seed, and the only SEO page that competitors cannot fake. Include the auditable-claim demo (top 20 claims).
3. **Week 2–3 — chart view (progressive layers + 22 character cards) free + glossary v0 (~30 entries: 10 十神, condensed 22 干支, 6 interactions).** Enough surface for "what is 七杀"-type long-tail. Explicitly a content layer, not the acquisition plan.
4. **Week 3–4 — one ritual artifact end-to-end: 满月 poster** (print-to-PDF, $19–29, gift-by-URL). Then run the distribution test the plan only gestures at: 10 real diaspora friends/family, measure forwards and redemptions.
5. **Day 30 — decision gate with named numbers.** ≥3 paid 合婚 reports = wedge signal; poster forwards per buyer ≥1.5; glossary entries → chart-view rate. If 合婚 converts and literacy surfaces don't, flip the plan to engine+ritual-first with literacy as layer, and restate odds honestly.

**MAY:** reuse family corpus only with consent; manual fulfillment while volume <10/mo; auditable-claims for top 20 only. **NEVER:** family charts on the landing page; mainland launch; single-scalar compatibility scores; health/death/money verdicts (unchanged).

## 8. Calibrated verdict

| Outcome | As sequenced (literacy-first MVP) | Resequenced (paid ritual wedge first) |
|---|---|---|
| Valuable (≥$2k/mo within 18 mo) | ~20% | ~40% |
| Niche-viable but marginal | +5–10% | +15% |
| Dead within 12 mo (no revenue wedge found) | ~40% | ~20% |
| Perpetual hobby | ~30% | ~25% |

Salvageable: yes, structurally — the engine, intake, chart view, and lexicon carry over under any strategy, so P(assets reused | failure) ≈ 90%. The kill risk isn't the product; it's spending the next four months building a school for students who haven't enrolled, while the paying customers wait at surface 6.

One sentence: **the plan is right about the engine, right about the URL, right about honesty-as-trust — and wrong about what to build first; it demoted its own best bet, and the fix costs two weeks.**
